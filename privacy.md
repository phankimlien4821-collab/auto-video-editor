# Privacy Policy for Auto Video Editor

Effective date: November 25, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Auto Video Editor is a desktop application designed to help users edit videos, generate captions and publish authorized content to Facebook Pages and YouTube channels.

## 2. Information We Access

The application may access Facebook Page IDs, Page names, access tokens, YouTube channel information, video files, captions, descriptions and publishing information authorized by the user.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information is used to connect authorized accounts, edit videos, generate content descriptions, publish videos and verify publishing status.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Video processing is performed locally on the user's computer. Account access tokens are stored locally using Windows encryption.

Users should keep their access tokens and API credentials confidential.

## 5. Third Party Services

The application integrates with Meta Graph API, YouTube Data API and Google Gemini API.

When users enable these features, relevant information is transmitted to the selected service to perform the requested operation. These services are subject to their respective privacy policies.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can disconnect their accounts through the application's account management settings and revoke application permissions through Facebook or Google.

Users may request assistance with deleting application data by contacting the developer.

## 7. User Rights

Users can manage their connected accounts, revoke permissions and request access to or deletion of their personal information.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when application features or data processing practices change.
